A lively concert built around all living things.  We'll have marches like Invincible Eagle and Call of the Elk, some unique selections like the 1912 Donkey Serenade and Blue Goose Rag to some great medleys like the Beatles and How to Train Your Dragon.  We will be supporting the Humane Society this week with a free-will offering and collection of food and treats.  Join our Mayor Nickels as the MMB kicks off their summer season.
